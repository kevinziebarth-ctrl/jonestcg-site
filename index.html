Who we are

What we do

The Jones TCG is a Tampa card business run by two partners. We bring sealed Pokémon and Magic product, graded slabs and singles to card shows, and we're always buying and trading.

Buy

Booster boxes, ETBs, collection boxes and singles.

Sell

We buy sealed, graded slabs, binders and whole collections.

Trade

Sell us your cards

Message us before a show and we'll have an offer ready when you get to the table.

  1. Snap a few photosFronts and backs for singles, the label for slabs, all sides for sealed. A pile shot works for a big collection.
  2. Tell us your priceHave a number in mind? Send it, it helps us move fast. Not sure? Just say so and we'll make you an offer.
  3. Get an offerWe reply with a price or a time to meet at our next show.
